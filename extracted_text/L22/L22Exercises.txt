Why recoverable and cascadeless?

The write of T2 can be ignored (called a blind
write). It is not preceded by a read, and it is
immediately overwritten by T1. So, its value is
never seen by any transaction.

Recoverable: If Tj reads a data item written by Ti and Tj commits after Ti.
Cascadeless: If Tj reads a data item written by Ti and Ti commits before read of Tj.
